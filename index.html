Captain Safety Policy

Safety guidelines for Heartifo Captains

1. Purpose

The safety of Captains, customers and other road users is an important part of the Heartifo platform.

This policy explains the basic safety responsibilities expected from Heartifo Captains.

2. Safety First

Captains should always prioritize safety over completing a ride or reaching a destination quickly.

  • Follow applicable traffic laws.
  • Drive responsibly and attentively.
  • Follow reasonable road safety practices.
  • Do not knowingly put customers or others at unnecessary risk.

3. Safe Vehicle

Captains are responsible for keeping their vehicle in a reasonably safe operating condition.

  • Maintain important vehicle components.
  • Use required safety equipment.
  • Do not knowingly operate an unsafe vehicle.
  • Keep required vehicle documents valid.

4. Responsible Driving

Captains must drive in a manner that is consistent with applicable traffic and road safety requirements.

  • Avoid reckless or dangerous driving.
  • Follow speed limits and traffic signals.
  • Maintain appropriate attention to the road.
  • Use appropriate care in difficult traffic or weather conditions.

5. Mobile Phone Use

Captains should not use a mobile phone in a manner that distracts them while driving.

Any navigation or communication should be handled in a way that does not create an unnecessary safety risk.

6. Alcohol and Drugs

Captains must not operate a vehicle while impaired by alcohol, drugs or any other substance that may affect their ability to drive safely.

7. Customer Safety

Captains should treat customer safety as a priority throughout the ride.

  • Follow the appropriate pickup and drop-off process.
  • Communicate respectfully with customers.
  • Do not engage in threatening or abusive behaviour.
  • Do not intentionally create unsafe situations.

8. Customer Information

Customer phone numbers, addresses, ride details and other personal information must be handled responsibly.

Such information should only be used for legitimate purposes connected with the Heartifo service.

9. Unsafe Customer Situations

If a Captain believes that continuing a ride would create a genuine safety risk, the Captain should prioritize safety and use the appropriate Heartifo support or cancellation process.

10. Accidents

If an accident occurs, Captains should first take reasonable steps to protect themselves, customers and other people.

The incident should be reported to Heartifo as soon as reasonably possible.

11. Emergency Situations

In an emergency, Captains should contact the appropriate local emergency services when necessary.

Heartifo Support should also be contacted as soon as reasonably possible after immediate safety needs have been addressed.

12. Safety Equipment

Captains should use and maintain safety equipment required by applicable law and relevant vehicle requirements.

13. Safety Reports

Captains should promptly report relevant safety incidents, accidents, threats or serious concerns to Heartifo.

Heartifo may request additional information to understand and review the incident.

14. Safety Violations

Serious or repeated safety violations may result in account review, temporary restrictions, suspension or deactivation under applicable Heartifo policies.

15. Captain Responsibility

Captains remain responsible for complying with applicable traffic, vehicle, licensing and other legal requirements.

16. Policy Changes

Heartifo may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, applicable requirements or operational practices.

17. Contact Heartifo

For safety concerns, accident reporting or Captain safety support, please contact Heartifo.
